Privacy Policy

Cat Rogue (iOS app) ・ 日本語

Pokke Games (the "Developer") handles user information in the iOS app "Cat Rogue" (the "App") as follows.

1. Information the Developer collects

The Developer does not collect personal information such as your name, email address or phone number. No account is required.

Game progress (your current journey, records, the codex, shrine unlocks and settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to the Developer's servers. Deleting the App deletes this data.

2. Advertising

To offer the App free of charge, it uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. AdMob may collect and use the following information to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud:

  • The advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking in your device settings
  • Your IP address and the approximate location estimated from it
  • Device type, OS version, and information about app interactions and ad impressions or taps
  • Crash and performance diagnostics

Please see Google's policies for how this information is handled.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

3. App Tracking Transparency

The App may ask for permission to use the advertising identifier through iOS App Tracking Transparency in order to show ads that suit you. You can use every feature of the App free of charge even if you do not allow it. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. Consent management

In some regions the App may ask for consent to the use of information for advertising through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad consent in the App.

5. Children

The App is suitable for all ages. Ads shown in the App are limited to general-audience (G) content.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when necessary. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the support page.
